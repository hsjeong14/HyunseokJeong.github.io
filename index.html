About

My research focuses on the intersection of artificial intelligence, medical imaging, and real-time guidance technologies, with the goal of enhancing diagnostic accuracy and accessibility in clinical practice. I am currently a postdoctoral research fellow at the Division of Computer Engineering, Hankuk University of Foreign Studies, working with Prof. Byunghwan Jeon.

Scan Guide Software System Commercialized

2023 – 2025 · The Ministry of Science and ICT

A deep learning-based solution for real-time image quality assessment and probe motion guidance, enabling novice operators to obtain standardized cardiac ultrasound images with reduced inter-operator variability.

Role: Managed practical operations of the project and led software development of the echocardiography Scan Guide system. The related paper is published in an SCI journal.

AI-Gatekeeper Solution: Chest X-Ray Module

2022 – 2024 · The Ministry of Science and ICT

Predicting Coronary Artery Calcium Score (CACS) from chest X-rays, enabling early severity assessment that typically requires CT. Explainable predictions from routine imaging may reduce unnecessary invasive procedures and improve clinical decision-making.

Role: Managed practical operations and led development of the chest X-ray disease prediction module. Two papers have been published in SCI journals.

[No. 1711139017] Development of Various-type Feature-based Artificial Intelligence Gatekeeper Solution for the Screening of Coronary Artery Disease
